INTRODUCTION  Modern  helicopter  design  requires  a  knowledge  of  rotor  performance  characteristics  at  high  forward  speeds.  Theoretical  predictions  have  not  been  substantiated  in  this  area,  and  only  a  limited  amount  of  experimental  data  has  been  available.  encountered  at  high  speed,  many  of  the  assumptions  and  approximations  of  rotor  theory  become  questionable.  Questions  arise  regarding  not  only  the  magnitudes  of  rotor  forces  and  moments,  but  also  the  character  of  rotor  operation,  for  example,  flapping  stability  at  advance  ratios  as  high  as  1.0.
